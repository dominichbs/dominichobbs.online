About Dom

I built this from scratch because nobody handed it to me.
That means I know exactly what's stopping you.

I'm Dominic Hobbs, a fitness coach based in the UK working with clients online and in person. I've served in the military, competed athletically at a high level, and held demanding full time careers, all while maintaining my physique and performance.

I've also come back from severe surgeries that genuinely changed my life. Every time I rebuilt. Not because of talent, but because of mindset. That's the mechanism everything else follows from.

Build a strong, aesthetic body. Move like an athlete, not just look like one. Train with purpose. No guesswork.
